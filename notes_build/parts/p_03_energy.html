3.1 Must-know summary

TermMeaning / rule
WorkWork = force × distance moved in the direction of the force. Unit: joule (J). Zero work is done when there is no displacement (pushing a wall) or when the force is perpendicular to the displacement (carrying a book while walking horizontally, a runner completing one lap of a circular track: displacement is zero). Work is positive if the force acts in the direction of motion and negative if it opposes it.
EnergyCapacity to do work (J). Kinetic energy = ½ m v²; gravitational potential energy = m g h. Law of conservation: energy cannot be created or destroyed, only changed from one form to another.
PowerRate of doing work: P = W / t = F v. Unit: watt (W). The same work done in different times gives different powers.
EfficiencyEfficiency = (useful energy output / total energy input) × 100 %. It is always less than 100 % because some energy is wasted as heat and sound.
Energy efficiencyDoing the same job with less energy. Methods: efficient (5-star / LED) appliances, insulation, switching off, natural lighting, good design. Benefits: lower electricity bills, lower demand and imports, fewer emissions, resources conserved.
Voltage dropThe fall in voltage along wires because of their resistance (heat I²R is wasted). Minimising it (thick, short wires; good joints; higher-voltage transmission) saves energy and keeps machines at full efficiency and safe.
Energy resourcesRenewable (solar, wind, hydro) reduce dependence on fossil fuels and emissions. Bhutan depends on hydropower, which is low in winter, so solar adds energy security.
FormulaUse
W = F d    P = W/t = F v    KE = ½ m v²    GPE = m g hwork, power, energies
Efficiency = (output / input) × 100 %machines, appliances, see-saw, transformer

3.3 Solved questions — Section B

2023 Q3(b) || A mother lifts a 15 kg baby to 2 m in 2 s. Find the power. || Work = m g h = 15 × 10 × 2 = 300 J. Power = W / t = 300 / 2 = 150 W (147 W if g = 9.8). 2023 Q4(b) || Motorcycle A has a greater engine force than B but both reach the same velocity. Which has more power? || P = F v. With the same v, the larger force gives the larger power, so motorcycle A has more power. 2023 Q8(b) || Design an energy-efficient house for a cold place using methods of energy efficiency. || See Fig 3.1: thick insulated walls and roof (wool/foam/cavity); double-glazed, small windows on the cold side and large south-facing windows to gain sunlight; a draught-proofed door with a porch; stone/concrete floor that stores heat; solar water heater/panels; efficient heating (bukhari/heat recovery); LED lamps, 5-star appliances; thick curtains. 2023 Q8(e) || How does a high voltage drop in a circuit affect the efficiency of a machine? || A large voltage drop means much electrical energy is wasted as heat in the wires and the machine gets a lower voltage than needed, so its output is less and its efficiency decreases. 2023 Q9(c) || Bhutan should not depend on hydropower alone. Explain one alternative source with THREE minimal environmental impacts. || Solar energy. (1) No greenhouse gases or air pollution when generating electricity. (2) Rooftop panels need no dam or new land, so rivers, forests and wildlife are not disturbed. (3) No fuel, no waste and no noise during operation; it is renewable and complements hydropower in winter when river flow is low. 2024 Q2(a) || TWO benefits of energy-efficient appliances. || (1) They use less electricity, so bills are lower. (2) Lower energy demand means fewer emissions and less use of natural resources (and a longer life for the appliance). 2024 Q4(b) || Significance of minimising voltage drop in an electric design. || It reduces energy loss as heat in the wires, keeps the supply voltage at the appliances correct so they work properly, avoids overheating and improves efficiency. 2024 Q4(c) || Design an energy-efficient classroom. || Efficient appliances: LED lamps, 5-star fans and computers, occupancy/motion sensors and switches. Insulation: insulated roof and walls, double-glazed windows, draught-proofed doors. Technology: solar panels / solar water heater, smart meters, timers. Design: large windows for daylight and cross-ventilation, light-coloured walls, trees for shade, rainwater harvesting. Result: less energy and waste, resources conserved, minimal environmental impact. 2024 Q5(b) || A 300 W electric wheelchair (50 kg) with a 50 kg occupant climbs a 10 m high ramp at steady velocity. Time? || Work = m g h = (50 + 50) × 10 × 10 = 10 000 J. Time = W / P = 10 000 / 300 = 33.3 s. 2024 Q7(c) || Tug of war: Team A pulls 200 N, Team B 1000 N. Which team did positive work? || Team B: its force acts in the direction of the displacement of the rope (the rope moves towards B). Team A's force opposes the motion, so it does negative work. 2024 Q9(d) || A 1200 kg car at 15 m/s brakes and its speed is halved. Find the KE. || New speed = 7.5 m/s. KE = ½ m v² = 0.5 × 1200 × 7.5² = 600 × 56.25 = 33 750 J. 2025 Q2(b) || A 250 g ball is thrown up at 30 m/s; find its KE at 2 s using the graph. || From the graph, v at 2 s = 10 m/s. KE = ½ m v² = 0.5 × 0.25 × 10² = 12.5 J. 2025 Q5(a) || A man climbs a slope; another walks the same distance on level ground. Who expends more energy? || The man climbing the slope: besides overcoming friction he does work against gravity, raising his weight through a height (gaining m g h of potential energy). 2025 Q7(f) || Why is Bhutan investing in solar power (250 kW and 500 kW plants)? || Hydropower is low in winter (dry season), forcing imports; solar is strong then, so it gives energy security and reduces imports. Solar is clean and renewable (supports carbon neutrality), quick to build, suits rooftops and rural areas, and diversifies energy sources. 2025 Q9(b) || See-saw: A = 750 N, B = 450 N; B is lifted 0.5 m when A sits down. Efficiency? || Equal arms, so A moves down 0.5 m. Work output (lifting B) = 450 × 0.5 = 225 J. Work input (A's weight) = 750 × 0.5 = 375 J. Efficiency = 225 / 375 × 100 = 60 %.
