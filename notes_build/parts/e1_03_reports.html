Chapter 3: Report WritingQ3 · 10–15 marks · asked in 2023 (picture), 2024 (facts table) and 2025 (programme)

3.1 Must-know summary

FeatureWhat to do
PurposeTo give an accurate, factual account of an event (or a picture) for readers of a newspaper, school magazine or club.
HeadlineShort, catchy, in capital or title case: Blood Donation Camp Held at Gyelzang HSS.
By-line & datelineBy Pema, Executive Reporter, Nyetshuel  |  Thimphu, 14 November 2024: (place + date).
Paragraph 1 (lead)Answer the 5Ws + H: What happened, Who organised/took part, Where, When, Why (purpose), and How.
Middle paragraphsDetails in order: programme, numbers, activities, what officials/participants said (reported speech).
Last paragraphResult / impact / what happens next / thanks.
Tense & voicePast tense (the camp was organised) • often passive • third person • no personal opinion (I think, I felt).
Word limit2023: ~150 words • 2024 & 2025: 180–200 words. Use all the given information (names, numbers, date, purpose).
Picture-based reportDescribe what you can see (who/what/where), infer the cause (heavy rain → landslide), add reasonable details (officials, action taken), keep it realistic.

3.4 Solved reports

2023 Q3Picture report (about 150 words) — Kencho, reporter, News Today, Zhemgang. [15]

Landslide Blocks Highway; Travellers Stranded

By Kencho, News Today, Zhemgang

Zhemgang: A massive landslide blocked a section of the highway in Zhemgang yesterday, leaving many vehicles and passengers stranded for several hours.

The landslide occurred early in the morning after heavy rain loosened the soil on the steep hillside. Large rocks, mud and uprooted trees spilled across the road and completely cut off the traffic. A car got stuck in the thick mud, while a bus and other vehicles waited helplessly on the far side. Some passengers walked carefully over the slippery debris to see how bad the situation was.

Soon after receiving the information, a team from the Department of Roads reached the spot with an excavator and began clearing the debris. The workers also warned people to stay away from the unstable slope. Officials said that the road would be opened to light vehicles by evening, and they advised drivers to travel carefully during the monsoon.

2024 Q3Blood donation camp (180–200 words) — Pema, executive reporter of Nyetshuel. [15]

Blood Donation Camp Held at Gyelzang Higher Secondary School

By Pema, Executive Reporter, Nyetshuel

Thimphu, 14 November 2024: A one-day blood donation camp was organised by Jigme Dorji Wangchuck National Referral Hospital, Thimphu, at Gyelzang Higher Secondary School today to facilitate the voluntary contribution of blood from eligible students and teachers.

A total of 180 donors, including 150 students and 30 teachers, took part in the camp. The programme began in the morning with a short talk by a doctor, who explained the importance of blood donation and how one donation can save several lives. Donors were first checked for weight, haemoglobin level and blood pressure, and only healthy and eligible persons were allowed to donate. Trained nurses collected the blood under hygienic conditions, and the donors were given refreshments and rest afterwards.

The principal said that blood donation is a noble act and thanked the hospital team for visiting the school. A Class X student who donated for the first time said that he felt proud to help save lives. The blood collected will be sent to the hospital's blood bank for patients who need transfusion, such as accident victims and mothers during childbirth. The hospital team urged everyone to donate blood regularly.

2025 Q3Tree plantation programme (180–200 words) — Sonam, UNESCO Club coordinator, Gadhen Central School, Trashigang. [10]

UNESCO Club Plants Trees to Protect the Environment

By Sonam, UNESCO Club Coordinator, Gadhen Central School

Trashigang, 21 June 2025: The UNESCO Club of Gadhen Central School organised a tree plantation programme in the nearby community on Saturday to increase the green cover and to create awareness about protecting the environment.

About sixty students, teachers and community members took part in the programme. The Dzongkhag Forest Office provided 300 saplings of blue pine, cypress and walnut and guided the participants on the correct way of planting. The programme began with a short talk by the local Gup, who reminded everyone that trees prevent soil erosion and landslides, keep the air clean and provide shelter to wildlife. The volunteers then dug pits on a barren slope above the village, planted the saplings, watered them and protected each one with a fence of bamboo and branches.

Every club member adopted a sapling and promised to look after it throughout the year. The villagers appreciated the effort of the students and offered to help in watering the plants during the dry season. The club plans to monitor the saplings regularly and to organise more such programmes in the future.

Mistakes to avoid• Writing a story or personal diary (I woke up…) • Missing the headline/by-line • Leaving out a given fact (e.g. 150 students and 30 teachers) • Mixing tenses • Writing under/over the word limit.

Chapter 4: Table to Paragraphs (Data Interpretation)Q4 · 10 marks · not more than 150 words · asked in 2025

4.1 Must-know summary

StepWhat to do
1. Read the tableTitle, units (%), years, categories, source. Here: Enrolment in Secondary Education by Age — right age (IX = 14 yrs, X = 15 yrs), overage, underage for 2022 and 2023, plus the average.
2. IntroductionOne sentence: what the table shows, which years/classes.
3. Main bodyGroup the data: one paragraph per category. Quote key figures, compare classes and years (increase / decrease).
4. ConclusionOverall trend in one sentence.
5. RulesPresent or past tense (past for past years) • formal tone • no personal opinion • no copying every number — pick the important ones • stay under 150 words.
Describing changeWords
Upincreased / rose / went up / grew — from 15.50% to 24.15%
Downdecreased / fell / dropped / declined
Size of changeslightly • steadily • sharply • considerably
Comparinghigher / lower than • whereas • in contrast • while • the majority / a small proportion

4.2 Solved question

2025 Q4Study the table "Enrolment in Secondary Education by Age" and transfer the information into paragraphs using not more than 150 words. [10]

The table shows the percentage of Class IX and X students who were of the right age, overage and underage in 2022 and 2023. The right age is 14 years for Class IX and 15 for Class X.

Right-age students increased in both classes: from 15.50 to 24.15 per cent in Class IX and from 18.12 to 20.94 per cent in Class X, raising the average from 16.81 to 22.54 per cent.

In contrast, overage students declined. They fell from 82.68 to 72.48 per cent in Class IX and from 79.32 to 75.26 per cent in Class X. Underage students remained very few, though their average rose slightly from 2.59 to 3.18 per cent.

Overall, more students entered secondary school at the proper age in 2023, but overage students were still the majority.
