  • Frustum (truncated cone) = big cone − small cone, using similar triangles to find the missing height. Composite solids: add the volumes; for surface area add the exposed faces only (do not count faces joined together).
  • Efficiency: for the same volume the solid with the smaller surface area is more efficient (less material); for the same surface area the solid with the larger volume is more efficient. Cubes and cylinders with h = 2r are the most efficient prisms and cylinders.
  • Scaling: if the radius of a cylinder doubles (height same) the volume becomes 2² = 4 times. Displaced volume = base area × rise in water level.
  • 1 m³ = 1 000 000 cm³; 1 mL = 1 cm³; 1 L = 1000 cm³.
