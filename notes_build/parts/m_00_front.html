How marks are given (all three papers)

  • Section A (40 marks): Q1 = 20 MCQs, 2 marks each. Circle one letter. Many questions need a figure, a graph or a table, so practise reading them.
  • Section B (60 marks): attempt any SIX of Q2–Q9 (10 marks each). Choose the questions you can finish: constructions, graphs and calculations are quickest to score.
  • Show every step: formula → substitution → answer with unit. Marks are given for the method even if the last step is wrong.
  • Graphs: ruled axes with scale, label, plotted points ×, smooth curve or ruled line, title. Constructions: keep all arcs and construction lines, label points, state the measurement.
  • “Justify / explain”: give a reason with a number from the question. Formulae page: the formula sheet is printed on page 33 of the paper — you do not need to memorise the volume and surface-area formulas, but you must know how to use them.
