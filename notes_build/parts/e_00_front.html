How marks are given

  • “TWO points / THREE reasons”: one mark per correct point with a short explanation. Write exactly the number asked, in separate lines or numbered.
  • “Justify”, “Do you agree?”, “Do you think”: first write your stand (Yes / No / partly), then the reasons. Use the idea from the textbook plus a Bhutanese example.
  • Diagrams: axes with names (Quantity / Wage rate / Price), curves labelled D, S, new curve D1 or S1, E and E1, dotted lines to the axes, and one line to say what happened to price and quantity.
  • Calculations: formula, substitution, answer with unit (Nu, %, kg). Graphs: scale, labels, title, plotted points and bars.
  • “Differentiate” needs a two-column table with points that can be compared (not two separate definitions).
