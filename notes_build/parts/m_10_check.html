Last-day checklist
tick when you can do it without looking
  • Surds & finance: simplify and combine radicals; entire radicals; compound interest A = P(1 + r/n)nt (find r by taking the root); dividend DA = fv × r × n; yield = DA / OI; premium; compare rates by effective annual rate.
  • Linear: write models (M = 2500 − 500w); slope and y-intercept from a graph or equation; solve two equations (substitution / elimination); age, perimeter and points problems; function = one output per input.

  • Matrices: same order to add; multiplication rule and order; cost / points tables; A(B + C) vs (B + C)A; adjacency matrix and M² for one stop-over trips.
  • Trig: special-angle table; ratios from a given ratio; quadrants and signs; radians; identities and proofs (1 + cot² = cosec²); graphs of sin, cos, tan.
  • Solids: cylinder, cone, pyramid, sphere, prism; composite and frustum; displaced volume; efficiency (smaller SA for the same volume); scale factor (radius × 2 → volume × 4).
  • Geometry: mirror lines and turn symmetry of regular polygons; centroid, circumcentre (right and obtuse triangles); construct a triangle and its circumcircle; triangle and exterior angles.
  • Data: five-number summary and quartiles; grouped-data median; box plots and skew; double stem-and-leaf; scatter plot and line of best fit; correlation words; effect of an outlier.
  • Probability: with and without replacement; conditional from a Venn diagram; two-event multiplication; dependent vs independent.
  • In answers: formula, substitution, answer with unit; draw graphs with scale and label; keep construction arcs; give a reason for “justify”.
