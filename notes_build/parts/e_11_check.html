Last-day checklist
tick when you can write or draw it without looking
  • Diagrams: equilibrium wage with excess supply and excess demand; shift of D (price and quantity both rise) and shift of S (wage falls, quantity rises); equilibrium interest rate; circular flow with saving and investment.
  • Labour: four types; derived demand; MP and MRP table (hire while MRP ≥ wage); factors of demand and of supply; dignity of labour.
  • Factor income: wage, rent, interest, profit; saving vs hoarding; four deposit accounts; gross vs net interest; SI and CI; profit = TR − TC; business ethics.
  • Bhutan’s economy: features; diversification; roles of public and private sectors; income = consumption + saving; ways to raise saving.
  • Public finance: direct vs indirect tax with examples; tax vs non-tax; current vs capital expenditure; budget and supplementary budget and their importance.
  • Growth: economic vs non-economic, intermediate vs final, public vs private goods; GDP vs GNP; growth vs development; real vs money flow.
  • GNH and indicators: HDI (longevity, education, GDP per capita); 4 pillars and 9 domains with examples; spillover; brain drain; pro-family approach.
  • BoP: current, capital, financial accounts; BoT vs BoP; surplus vs deficit; appreciation vs depreciation; exchange-rate sum; importance of foreign exchange.
  • Trade: free trade vs protectionism; SAARC (8), SAPTA, SAFTA, BIMSTEC (7), WTO (1995, Geneva); benefits and problems of cooperation; regional vs international.
  • Graphs: bar (GDP per capita), line (GDP per capita USD), pie (angles = value / total × 360°); scale, labels, title.
  • In answers: write exactly the number of points asked; state your stand first in “justify” questions; use a Bhutanese example; show formula and unit in sums.
