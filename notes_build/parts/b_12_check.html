Last-day checklist
tick when you can write or draw it without looking
  • Draw and label: prokaryotic cell (nucleoid!), nephron, chloroplast with light and dark reaction, reflex arc, mitosis and meiosis stages, ring experiment before/after, digestive system.
  • Transport: diffusion / facilitated / osmosis / active (energy, gradient, protein); hypotonic, isotonic, hypertonic for animal and plant cells; potato osmoscope with control.
  • Photosynthesis: equation; grana vs stroma; ATP + NADPH; factors and the three graphs; Elodea experiment variables; temperature optimum.
  • Plants: xylem vs phloem and their four cell types each; translocation, source and sink; girdling results; hormones (auxin/NAA/2,4-D, gibberellin, ethylene, ABA).
  • Digestion: enzyme, organ, end product for carbohydrate, protein, fat; bile, HCl, mucus; villi; H. pylori (cause, test, treatment, why in Bhutan); constipation; lifestyle diseases.
  • Blood: RBC, WBC, platelets, plasma; ABO donor–recipient chart; antibodies and antigens; O universal donor, AB universal recipient; blood typing test.
  • Kidney: filtration, selective reabsorption, secretion; ADH and aldosterone; PCT, loop of Henle (descending permeable to water), DCT, collecting duct; kidney-damaging habits.
  • Nervous: reflex pathway; spinal reflex does not use the brain first; computer analogy (4 pairs); steroid side effects (physical and psychological).
  • Genetics: mitosis vs meiosis table; 2n → n counts (12 → 6); crossing over; Punnett squares (Tt×tt, Tt×Tt, Ee×ee, carrier × normal); incomplete dominance vs codominance; sex-linked.
  • Evolution: natural selection steps; peppered moth; four isolation types; bottleneck and founder effect; gene flow; Hardy–Weinberg (p + q = 1, q = 0.3); artificial selection; genetic engineering steps; IVF; anaerobic digestion and bioremediation.
  • Ecology and health: biotic vs abiotic; three levels of biodiversity; hotspot; Bhutan conservation (60% forest, protected areas); invasive species (lantana, crofton weed, polka dot) and control; HIV spread and stigma; COVID preparation.
  • In answers: count the marks, write that many points; label diagrams with ruled lines; define the variable (independent / dependent / controlled); always justify with “because…”.
