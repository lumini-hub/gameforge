How marks are given (all three papers)

  • Section A (40 marks): Q1 only — (a) 25 MCQs, (b) 5 fill-in-the-blanks, (c) 5 true/false, (d) 5 matching. Do all of it; no negative marking, but circle only ONE letter.
  • Section B (60 marks): attempt any SIX of Q2–Q9 (10 marks each, split into parts of 0.5–3 marks). Choose the questions with figures you can read.
  • “Label / identify”: write the exact biological name — one word is enough.
  • “Draw and label”: large, pencil, ruled label lines, a title, and only the parts asked. Graphs: title, axes labelled with units, even scale, points marked ×, smooth curve or ruled line.
  • “Justify / explain / comment”: claim + reason (“because…”). “Do you agree?”: start Yes/No, then give the reasons asked for.
  • Variables: independent = what you change; dependent = what you measure; controlled = what you keep the same.
