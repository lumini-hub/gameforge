Radicals (surds): rules

  • Product / quotient: a × b = ab; a ÷ b = a|b. A radical is simplified when no square (or cube) factor is left under the root: 50 = 52; ∛40 = 2∛5.
  • Like surds add and subtract: 8y-type terms are first simplified, then the like surds are combined.
  • Entire radical: put the number under the root: ab = a²b (35 = 45).
  • Powers of a root: xn = xn/2; √(2m) is a whole number when m is even and contains a radical when m is odd.
  • Perfect cube: every prime exponent is a multiple of 3.
