5.1 Must-know summary

Rule of thumbHomophones sound alike but have different spellings and meanings (weather/whether). Confused words look or sound similar (accept/except). Word-form pairs are the same root in a different part of speech (confident = adjective, confidence = noun). First decide the part of speech needed in the blank, then choose.

Chapter 6: Subject–Verb Agreement & Error Spotting2023 Q7 [10] · 2024 Q5 [5], Q8 [5] · 2025 Q9 [10] — the highest-scoring repeated topic

6.1 Must-know summary: agreement rules

#RuleExample (from the papers in bold)
1The verb agrees with the head noun, not the noun next to it.The slaughter of tigers for their bones and fur has caused controversy. • The presence of certain bacteria in our bodies is…
2Neither…nor / either…or / not only…but also: the verb agrees with the nearer subject.Neither the lab assistant nor the students were able…
3Each, every, everyone, either, neither, one + singular verb.Each person who climbs… mentions… • Each of the players has…
4Collective nouns (group, team, class, committee) are usually singular.Does that group of scientists think…?
5as well as / along with / together with / in addition to do not make the subject plural.Kuenden, as well as his classmates, was expecting…
6A pair of / a number of / a bunch of: "A pair of shoes is enough" (pair = singular). "A number of students are" (plural), "The number of students is" (singular).Example in 2023 Q7
7"One of the + plural noun" → verb is singular; but "one of the factors that determine" → verb after that is plural.One of the factors that determine our health.
8Uncountable nouns (news, information, advice, furniture) have no plural and no "a/an".He was expecting great news (not a great news).
9Singular-looking nouns: news, mathematics, physics, measles; plural-only: scissors, trousers, people, police.The news is good. • The police are here.
