Priority list (appears EVERY year) Expository essay • formal letter • report • error identification (correct/incorrect or find-the-error) • phrasal verbs / idioms / discourse markers (at least one every year) • MCQs on modals, parallelism, homophones and question tags.
Rules printed on the papers (never break them) • Do NOT write your real name, school, or any detail that discloses your identity — the answer may be cancelled. Use only the names given in the question (Dhendup, Sonam, Lhamo…).
• MCQs: circle only ONE letter — two circles = zero marks.
• Cloze / rearranging: use only ONE word per blank; spelling errors lose marks. Rearrange questions need all given words.
• Error-identification: correct only the error; do not rewrite the whole sentence.
