How to answer

  • One point = one mark. “TWO reasons” [2] → two separate, explained points. Extra points earn nothing.
  • Agree / Do you think… Justify: state Yes or No first, then the reasons asked. Link each reason to Bhutan.
  • Calculations: write the formula, substitute, give the unit (e.g. 4 min per degree).
  • Map work: use a pencil, thin neat lines, names in small capitals; use the symbol the question asks for (thick line, dot, ∧∧∧, ][, DZ, OP).
  • Do not write your name or school. Circle ONE letter only.
