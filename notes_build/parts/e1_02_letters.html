Chapter 2: Formal LetterQ2 · 15 marks · one of two situations · asked in 2023, 2024 and 2025

2.1 Must-know summary

PartWhat to write
Sender's addressTop (right or left): the writer's designation, organisation and place. Use only the details given in the question (e.g. Principal, Pemacholing Central School, Dagana).
DateBelow the sender's address, e.g. 12 September 2023.
Receiver's addressDesignation (The Manager / The Regional Transport Officer), organisation, place — on the left.
SalutationDear Sir/Madam, (Dear Sir, if the officer is clearly male). Never "Hi" or first names.
Subject lineOne line that states the purpose: Request for supply of items for school variety show.
BodyPara 1: purpose of writing   Para 2: details / reasons / items / dates   Para 3: clear request or action wanted + polite closing.
ClosingYours faithfully, (when you used "Dear Sir/Madam") + signature + name + designation.
LanguageFormal, polite, no contractions (do not, not don't), no slang, short clear sentences. Phrases: I am writing to… • I would be grateful if you could… • kindly arrange… • I look forward to your reply.
LengthAbout 170–200 words (15 lines of the answer booklet). Every point in the question must be covered — examiners tick them off.

Four letter types that keep repeating1) Complaint / request for action • 2) Order / supply request (items + quantity + date + payment + transport) • 3) Information / promotion (explain a package, fee, benefits) • 4) Application / permission. For an order letter, the question tells you exactly which points to include — give each one its own sentence.

2.3 Solved letters

2023 Q2(a)Principal Dhendup writes to the Regional Transport Officer (RSTA) about the danger and commotion caused by heavy traffic outside the school gate. [15]

Principal
Pemacholing Central School
Dagana
12 September 2023

The Regional Transport Officer
Road Safety and Transport Authority (RSTA)
Regional Office

Dear Sir,

Subject: Request for traffic control measures outside the school gate

I am writing to bring to your kind notice the dangerous traffic situation outside the main gate of Pemacholing Central School, Dagana, and to request your urgent action.

In recent years a new settlement has come up near the school, and the number of vehicles, shops and people along the road has grown rapidly. As a result, the road in front of our gate is badly congested, especially between 8:00 and 9:00 in the morning and between 3:30 and 4:30 in the evening. Taxis, buses and private cars park carelessly, the pavement is blocked, and hundreds of students have to squeeze through the crowd and cross the road. Several near accidents have already occurred, and the younger children are most at risk.

I would therefore be grateful if you could (i) post a traffic officer near the gate during the peak hours, (ii) install speed breakers, a zebra crossing and warning signs, and (iii) mark a separate parking and drop-off area away from the school entrance.

I am confident that your prompt action will protect the lives of our children. I would be happy to meet you or your officials to discuss the matter further. Thank you for your kind attention.

Yours faithfully,
Dhendup
Principal, Pemacholing Central School, Dagana

2023 Q2(b)Principal Dhendup writes to the Manager, Druk Culture Store, Thimphu requesting supply of items for a school variety show (items, date of show, transport, payment). [15]

Principal
Pemacholing Central School
Dagana
12 September 2023

The Manager
Druk Culture Store
Thimphu

Dear Sir/Madam,

Subject: Request for supply of items for the school variety show

Our school is organising an annual variety show on 25 November 2023 to showcase the talents of our students in dance, music and drama. I am writing to request you to supply the following items, which are needed for the performances:

1. Thirty sets of gho and kira in different sizes for the folk dances; 2. Ten traditional masks and costumes for the mask-dance item; 3. Four dramyens, two drums and five pairs of cymbals for the music items; 4. Twenty kabneys and rachus; and 5. Stage props and costume jewellery for the drama.

As Dagana is far from Thimphu, kindly send the items by a goods truck so that they reach the school by 15 November 2023; the transport charges may be added to the bill. We shall make the payment through online bank transfer within a week of receiving the items against a proper bill. Please also send us a quotation in advance.

I look forward to your kind cooperation and a favourable reply. Thank you.

Yours faithfully,
Dhendup
Principal, Pemacholing Central School, Dagana

2024 Q2(a)Sonam of Peaceful Trails (Bumthang) writes to the Principal of Yeshey Central School about a new one-day educational tour package; the fee is reasonable and food is included. [15]

Sonam
Peaceful Trails, Tour Operators
Bumthang
4 October 2024

The Principal
Yeshey Central School
Bumthang

Dear Sir/Madam,

Subject: New educational tour package for students

I am writing on behalf of Peaceful Trails, a tour operating agency based in Bumthang, to inform you about our new educational tour package designed especially for school students.

The package offers a one-day visit to the holy sites of our dzongkhag, namely Jambay Lhakhang, Kurjey Lhakhang, Tamshing Lhakhang and Jakar Dzong. A trained local guide will explain the history, legends and religious importance of each place, so that the students learn about our culture in a real setting. The tour begins at 8:00 a.m. and ends at 5:00 p.m., and safe transport and first-aid support are provided.

The fee is very reasonable at Nu. 600 per student, and this amount includes a hot lunch and evening tea. We also offer free entry for one teacher for every ten students. I believe that this tour would be a valuable learning experience for your students.

If you are interested, kindly let us know a convenient date at least one week in advance. I would be glad to visit your school to explain the package in detail. Thank you for your time.

Yours faithfully,
Sonam
Peaceful Trails, Bumthang

2024 Q2(b)Tshering, STEM club captain, writes to the Manager of Natsho Enterprise, Gasa to place a supply order for a science exhibition (items, quantity, mode of payment, transportation). [15]

Tshering, Captain
STEM Club, Norzoe Academy
Gasa
8 October 2024

The Manager
Natsho Enterprise
Gasa

Dear Sir/Madam,

Subject: Order for equipment for the science exhibition

Our school is organising a science exhibition on 2 November 2024, and as the captain of the STEM club I have been asked to place a supply order for the equipment needed. I am writing to request you to supply the following items:

1. Fifteen electric circuit kits (bulbs, switches and holders); 2. Twenty packets of AA batteries; 3. Ten rolls of insulated copper wire; 4. Ten magnifying glasses; 5. Fifty sheets of chart paper; and 6. Ten packets of coloured marker pens.

Kindly deliver the items to our school by your own vehicle on or before 28 October 2024, as we need time to prepare the models. The school will make the payment by account-payee cheque on delivery after checking the items against your bill. Please send us a quotation of the total cost before delivery, and a discount would be much appreciated since the items are for educational use.

I would be grateful for your prompt reply and cooperation. Thank you.

Yours faithfully,
Tshering
Captain, STEM Club, Norzoe Academy, Gasa

2025 Q2(a)Lhamo (class captain) writes to the proprietor of Delek Electronics, Zhemgang: the smart screen stopped functioning within two weeks; ask for a free replacement with clear reasons. [15]

Lhamo, Class Captain
Class X, Trong Middle Secondary School
Zhemgang
20 September 2025

The Proprietor
Delek Electronics
Zhemgang

Dear Sir,

Subject: Request for replacement of a defective smart screen

I am writing on behalf of my classmates to inform you that the smart screen we bought from your shop on 2 September 2025 has stopped working, and to request you to replace it without any additional charge.

My classmates and I collected Nu. 45,000 from our own pocket money to buy this screen for our classroom. It was installed on 4 September, but within two weeks the picture began to flicker and then the screen went completely blank. We have used it only for lessons, under the care of our teachers, so the fault cannot be due to misuse. Since the screen is new and was covered by your warranty, I believe the problem is a manufacturing defect. Moreover, our teachers can no longer use it for presentations, which is badly affecting our studies.

I therefore request you to replace the screen with a new one within a week. A copy of the bill and the warranty card is enclosed. We will be happy to show the screen to your technician at any time.

I hope you will treat this matter as urgent and help us. Thank you for your kind understanding.

Yours faithfully,
Lhamo
Class Captain, Class X

2025 Q2(b)Lhamo has moved to a new neighbourhood and writes to the Manager of Healthy and Fit Sports Club, Zhemgang seeking approval to join, explaining her interest. [15]

Lhamo
Class X, Trong Middle Secondary School
Zhemgang
20 September 2025

The Manager
Healthy and Fit Sports Club
Zhemgang

Dear Sir/Madam,

Subject: Request for approval to join the sports club

I am writing to request your kind approval to join the Healthy and Fit Sports Club. I have recently moved to a house near your club, and I would very much like to become a member.

I have always been interested in sports, especially basketball and badminton, and I represented my class in several inter-class matches. Regular exercise keeps me active and helps me to concentrate better on my studies. As a class X student, I do not have enough opportunity to practise properly at school, and your club offers good facilities and trained coaches. I believe that joining the club would improve my skills as well as my fitness and discipline.

I can attend the club between 4:30 and 6:30 in the evening on weekdays and the whole morning on holidays, so my studies will not be affected. My parents have given their permission. I would be grateful if you could tell me about the membership fee, rules and timings, and the documents I need to submit.

I look forward to your favourable reply. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Yours faithfully,
Lhamo

Common mistakes in letters• Writing your real name/school → cancellation. • "Dear Sir/Madam" + "Yours sincerely" (mismatch: with Sir/Madam use faithfully). • Missing subject line. • Forgetting one of the required points (date, transport, payment, quantity). • Informal words (kids, stuff, gonna). • Not ending with a clear request.
