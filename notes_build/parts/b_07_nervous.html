Key ideas

  • The nervous system detects changes (stimuli) and produces a quick response using electrical signals. CNS = brain + spinal cord; PNS = nerves.
  • Neurons: sensory neuron (receptor → CNS), interneuron / relay neuron (inside CNS), motor neuron (CNS → effector). Parts of a neuron: dendrites, cell body (with nucleus), axon (with myelin sheath), axon terminals.
  • Brain: cerebrum (thinking, memory, voluntary actions), cerebellum (balance), medulla (heartbeat, breathing).
  • Receptors (sense organs: eye, ear, skin, nose, tongue) detect stimuli; effectors (muscles, glands) respond.

The reflex arc

[[fig:b_reflex|Reflex arc: hot stove → receptor → sensory neuron → interneuron (spinal cord) → motor neuron → effector (muscle).]]
  • Karma touches a hot stove and pulls his hand away: Receptor → Sensory neuron → Interneuron → Motor neuron → Effector → C [[P25 Q1a(xxi)]].
  • A natural (spinal) reflex is processed in the spinal cord, so it is very fast; the brain is not in the initial pathway (it is told afterwards) → “natural reflexes involve the brain in the initial response” is FALSE [[P25 Q1c(iii)]].

Steroids

  • Anabolic steroids are synthetic hormones like testosterone that build muscle and body mass. They are banned in sport because they give an unfair advantage and are harmful [[P25 Q3a]].
  • Physical side effects: infertility, shrunken testes, irregular periods, spotting, acne, liver damage, high blood pressure, heart disease, deep voice and facial hair in women. Muscle development is the intended effect, not a side effect → D [[P23 Q1a(xi)]].
  • Psychological side effects: mood swings, aggression (“roid rage”), depression, anxiety, irritability, addiction/dependence, paranoia [[P23 Q7a]].

Solved questions

1) CPU = brain and spinal cord (CNS) which process information; 2) Keyboard = receptors / sense organs (input); 3) Monitor = effectors (muscles, glands) that give the response; 4) Hard disk = memory in the brain (storage). Athletes may not use anabolic steroids because: (1) it gives an unfair advantage over athletes who compete honestly; (2) it has serious side effects (heart and liver damage, infertility, aggression and depression). A computer takes input from the keyboard/sensors, the CPU processes it and gives output on the screen/speaker, with storage. In the same way receptors collect stimuli, send electrical signals by sensory neurons to the brain/spinal cord (processing), which sends signals by motor neurons to effectors (response); memory is stored in the brain. Four psychological side effects of excessive steroid use: mood swings, aggression, depression, anxiety/irritability (also addiction, paranoia). (i) The receptor that works like a keyboard is the touch receptor of the skin (or any sense organ): it receives a stimulus and converts it into an electrical signal sent to the brain, as the keyboard turns key presses into signals for the CPU. (ii) A machine sensor (light, heat, sound, pressure sensor) detects a change and converts it to an electrical signal for processing, exactly like a receptor. (iii) Limitation: receptors cover only a limited range (we cannot see UV or hear very high sounds) and can be damaged/tired; sensors need power, can fail or be inaccurate. (iv) They help create robots, medical devices, artificial limbs, cameras and smart machines and help scientists understand the body.
